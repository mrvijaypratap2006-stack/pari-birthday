PARI BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — QUICK SETUP
==================================

1) Keep index.html beside the videos folder.
2) The website uses the rotated photo derivative, videos/pari-memory.jpg,
   for the hero background, book cover, and the first open-book spread.
   The cover features a close-cropped photo; the original photo remains
   unchanged in videos/.
3) The book opens to a short birthday letter with an original Hindi couplet,
   then turns through all 86 videos one at a time using browser-compatible
   copies from videos/web/. Video preview images are in videos/posters/.
   The final chapter is a birthday letter for Pari (Himanshi Shekhawat).
4) Open index.html in your browser, or use VS Code Live Server.
   A local server is recommended for the most reliable video playback.
5) To share or publish the website, include the complete Pari_Birthday
   folder. Do not send or upload index.html on its own.

The photo and videos are referenced from the videos folder rather than
embedded in the HTML. Original videos remain unchanged in videos/; the
website plays H.264 copies from videos/web/ for better phone and browser
compatibility. Preview frames are saved in videos/posters/.

Keep the entire videos folder, including its web/ and posters/ subfolders,
when sharing or publishing the website.
The originals and browser-ready copies together use about 421 MB.

The assets folder is not used by the current page.
